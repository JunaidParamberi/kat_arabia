01 — About KAT Arabia updated copy

International standards, local execution

KAT Arabia General Contracting is an engineering and contracting company serving government entities, EPC contractors, industrial operators and private sector clients. We are part of KAT Group, a diversified multinational business group headquartered in the United Arab Emirates, with offices in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah and Al Jubail.

We combine international standards with local execution to deliver reliable, sustainable and value-driven projects, from design and engineering through construction, commissioning and maintenance.
